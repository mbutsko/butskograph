Whether or not the universe is physically deterministic, I think that our thoughts, beliefs and attitudes are things that appear in our consciousness without us choosing them. For this reason, I don't believe we have free will in any sense, and I therefore don't believe in credit or blame for one's thoughts or actions. I think that [[Capitalism]] therefore falsely rewards people for things not of their own merit, which is inconsistent with itself. As a practical matter, it's pretty difficult to be live this belief, but I guess it doesn't matter because I don't think that I chose it.
