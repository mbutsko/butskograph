My favorite group sizes are 2 and 3. Beyond that, I find conversation gets harder and [[Anxiety & Depression]] increases. I am envious of people for whom social interaction recharges their batteries (like my daughter Maddie). For me, it has trended more difficult and less a part of my life over time. 

I have very few friends (like seriously 2) that I talk to on a regular basis outside of work. I see my extended family a few times a year, but I don't otherwise have any sort of community or tribe that I am a part of. That regularly makes me sad, but every time I have tried to engage in some new social group (most often fitness), I have immediately become anxious and self-conscious and withdrawn. Now I'm conditioned to expect that that's just how it is for me and it's an uphill battle even just to try.
