Sometime around the time of the pandemic, I started having unexplained stomach pain in response to certain foods. This pain is often so severe that I have to lie down and just want to sleep. It has caused me to eat less and over the course of the last year I've (unintentionally) lost 20.5 pounds. This is deeply intertwined with [[Compulsive, Disordered Eating]] and my complicated relationship with [[Animal Suffering & Veganism]]. 

Recently (August 2026) I took a Trio Smart Breath test which confirmed that I have very high levels of methane consistent with a condition called IMO ([Intestinal Methanogen Overgrowth](https://www.cedars-sinai.org/newsroom/gut-microorganism-may-play-a-role-in-constipation/) ). Its kind of nice to get some validation that it's a real problem (recognized and treated by gastroenterologists), but unfortunately not one that has a simple, lasting solution, so my journey continues.
