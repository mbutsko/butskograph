Starting around age 30 I started focusing more on my physical health and within 2 years I had completed a half marathon, full marathon and then two trail 50k races within a month in the winter of 2014. I injured my sesamoid the next year and basically gave up on running in favor of [[cycling]] in 2015, which was difficult because I really loved going for long runs. 

Long distance cycling actually seemed to make the foot injury worse for many years, but it didn't actually prevent my from cycling. In the Winter of 2024 I had cut way back on cycling and was able to ease back into running (scaled way back )
