I didn't care much about my health until the age of 28. Prior to that, I smoked cigarettes, never exercised, and only thought about my diet as it pertained to [[Animal Suffering & Veganism]]. My weight fluctuated quite a bit (+/- 70 lbs up and down). 

Over the course of my 30s I became interested in regularly getting bloodwork, seeing my [[Genetics]], regularly exercising and avoiding unhealthy foods (and reckoning with [[Sobriety & Addiction]]). While I've listened to lots of podcasts and watched lots of Youtube videos with lots of perspectives about health and diet and their impact on health, I'm not trying to live forever or avoid death. I am trying to avoid debilitating chronic illness and *premature* death (my biological mother died at age 53).  I want to be healthy and active for the rest of my life, but living to 100+ doesn't interest me.
