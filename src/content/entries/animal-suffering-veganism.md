We do not need to farm animals in the way that we do to maintain ourselves. The extent of the number of conscious beings that suffer unnecessarily to accommodate human preference (our desire for meat, cheese and eggs) is staggering. I can't fathom it. People are quick to defend this in 1000 ways, but it will never make sense to me. Eat any number of things that don't have to live in misery -- vegetables, nuts, seeds, beans, mushrooms, fruit, whole grains. Prefer those that can be grown close to home and harvested by people paid reasonably. These foods are largely inexpensive. They save land. The emit orders of magnitude less in greenhouse gasses, and most importantly, they don't require living beings to live and die in excruciating conditions.

With that said, I'm not _a Vegan_ and over time have adjusted making certain concessions for my health (I can't really eat beans or soy anymore due to my [[GI Health Woes]] and my [[Finger Pain]]). In my current rotation of foods with protein are mussels and Quorn pieces, which contain trace amounts of egg.
