I've accumulated more wealth than 99% of people who have ever lived. I participate in a capitalist system; I build wealth via my home, and my retirement accounts, savings accounts, college savings accounts. Capitalism has made me a winner at accumulation.

I don't buy any of this. I don't believe in [[Free Will]]. I don't work 99% harder than the rest of the earth, and I'm not owed any more than anyone else. I think that capitalism is a trap, and I would describe myself as an anti-capitalist who lacks the courage to exit the game. The game of accumulation teaches us to prioritize our own needs, and our own expected future needs above all else. 

Societies that prioritize the collective good (other people, other species, the planet) have plenty of suffering. People have _less_, but it doesn't mean that they do not have [[Enough]].
