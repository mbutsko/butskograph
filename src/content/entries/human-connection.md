I am envious of people for whom social interaction recharges their batteries (like my daughter Maddie). For me, it has trended more difficult and less a part of my life over time. 

I do have a fantasy that Jess and I retire to a proper mediterranean/Spanish lifestyle in Europe and drink wine with friends over tapas every night (unlikely).
