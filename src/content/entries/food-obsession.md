From the moment I wake up, I start thinking about resisting the urge to eat something. It's not exactly the urge _to eat something_ because I know that I will feel too guilty to swallow the food. I know exactly how much peanut butter, protein powder, frozen bananas, granola, cliff bars, almonds, cashews, mayonaise, are in the house at all times. Its not something we talk about much as a family, but it weighs on me a great deal.
