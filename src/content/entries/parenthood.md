Parenthood is asymmetric in multiple ways. First off, the love a parent has for a child is very different than the love a child has for their parent. Both of these are pretty distinct from the love we have for others in different contexts such as [[Marriage]] and [[Human Connection & Friendship]]. 

My children are amazing people in very different ways. I am proud of them every day and I feel guilty for the ways in which my proclivities affect them. I want them to be happy and I also want to be able to mindfully enjoy what time I have left with them under our roof.
