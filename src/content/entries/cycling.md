I have always loved riding bikes. As a kid I had many, many bikes and it was (as for many 70s-90s kids) my primary mode of transportation and recreation.

I first rode a bike *to work* around the age of 23. It was liberating when it occurred to me that I could combine this joy of cycling with the need to get places.

Starting around age 28 I rode to work every day, and abstained from having a parking pass. It wasn't a long commute, and it was kind of annoying at times, but it felt really good to cut back and simplify. 

After a [[Running]] injury in my 30s, I bought a road bike and starting doing endurance cycling (including a few century rides). I loved it, and I was obsessed with it, including commuting ~8miles to work every day. 

Over the last few years, I continued to have problems with hip, back and foot overuse injuries, so I tapered down to commuting only, and then in the Spring of 2026 a bad back injury caused me to give up the commute (for now!) in favor of running to the bus stop. I miss riding my bike to work every day; it was great for my mental health and I hope to be able to work it back in someday, in some fashion.
