I would consider myself a *strong atheist* in that I not only *don't believe* in God as an omnipotent/omniscient/omnibenevolent God, I actually believe there isn't such a being. I don't really get spirituality. I do not believe in [[Free Will]]. I am skeptical about historical accuracy. I think the infinite number of possible creation myths speaks against the probability of any of our known creation myths being accurate. Religion provides a lot of benefit for many people, but it also provides the foundation for an insane amount [[Animal Suffering & Veganism]]. It is inherently conservative (I am *progressive*, in case you couldn't tell). I don't need to go on and on, it just doesn't fit for me.
