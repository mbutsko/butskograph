We do not need to farm animals in the way that we do to maintain ourselves. The extent of the number of conscious beings that suffer unnecessarily to accommodate human preference (our desire for meat, cheese and eggs) is staggering. I can't fathom it. People are quick to defend this in 1000 ways, but it will never make sense to me. Eat any number of things that don't have to live in misery -- vegetables, nuts, seeds, beans, musrooms, fruit, whole grains. Prefer those that can be grown close to home and harvested by people paid reasonably. These foods are largely inexpensive. They save land. The emit orders of magnitude less in greenhouse gasses, and most importantly, they don't require living beings to live and die in excruciating conditions.

With that said, I'm not _a Vegan_. I make the following exceptions which slightly change over time as my understanding about my [[GI  Health Woes]] and my [[Finger Pain]] evolves. Some Vegans would argue that these are a *slippery slope*, and for many areas in my life (like my struggle with [[Sobriety & Addiction]]) I totally agree with the risks of slippery-slopey-ness, but for some reason these particular points have not devolved/degraded to anything that I would be ashamed to admit. These "rules" are current as of the last ~2 years and apply only *to my consumption* of animal-derived products:

1.) If someone is literally throwing meat away, I will eat it. I won't order it or cause it to be ordered on my behalf.
2.) I will occasionally eat eggs from Copia farms, a local mobile-pasture farm that treats its chickens in ways that I am comfortable with. By [[Genetics]] I have to be more careful then most about eating cholesterol, so I don't make a habit of this.
3.) I eat mussels and scallops because I am convinced that they do not suffer. 
4.) I am currently eating small amounts of low-fat Yogurt from Snowville Creamery; on net, I am not comfortable eating dairy in any setting due to the way we need to keep animals in lactation and the fate endured by the offspring of dairy-producing animals, but at the moment I think its the least bad food option that I have while I figure out some other health problems.
